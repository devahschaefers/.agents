ALWAYS create a worktree for your work (put it in `~/.worktrees/{repo name}/`). When the work is done, ask whether to merge to main or put up a PR.

Commit frequently, especially when doing large changes.

## Naming

Names are the most important thing in designing a good, readable codebase. When writing or reviewing code, be extra critical of every name: would someone with no context know exactly what this thing is just from reading its name?

This does not mean making names insanely long (though longer names are fine when they're genuinely needed). It means thinking deeply about what the thing actually *is*. There is usually one word, or a combination of words, that fits perfectly and describes it precisely. Sometimes that name is obvious; sometimes it takes more thought. Put in that thought.
